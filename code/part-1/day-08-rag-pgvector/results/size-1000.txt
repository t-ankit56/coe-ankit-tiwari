Q: How many leave days can I carry over, and when do they expire?
A: You can carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days to the next year, and these carried-over days expire on 31 March [1].

[1] leave-policy.md (distance 0.357)
    # Leave Policy (Fictional Co.) ## Annual leave Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. New joiners get a pro-rated amount based on their joining month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and carried-over days expire on 31 March. ## Sick leave Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is needed only when sick leave runs for more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over. ## Parental leave Primary caregivers get 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers get 4 weeks. Leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption. ## Applying for leave Leave requests go through the HR portal and are approved by the line manager. Requests for more than 5 consecutive days need 2 weeks of notice. Unapproved absence of 3 days or more is treated as a disciplinary matter.

A: You must report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 1 hour [1].
