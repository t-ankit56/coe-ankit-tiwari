# Expenses and Travel (Fictional Co.)

## Submitting expenses
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless Finance approves an exception. Claims under 25 USD do not need a receipt but need a short note.

## Travel
Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy. Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy. Hotels are capped at 180 USD per night in major cities and 120 USD elsewhere. Book through the travel portal; bookings made outside it are reimbursed only up to the portal price.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance on business trips is 60 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Client dinners are reimbursed up to 100 USD per person with the guest list written on the claim.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per year for courses, books and conferences. The budget does not roll over. Conference travel is paid from the travel budget, not the learning budget.
