# Remote Work Guide (Fictional Co.)

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing their 3-month probation. During probation, employees work from the office 5 days a week so mentors can pair with them.

## Core hours
Everyone must be reachable between 11:00 and 16:00 in their own time zone. Outside core hours people may arrange their day freely, but meetings should not be scheduled before 09:00 or after 18:00.

## Home office allowance
A one-time home office allowance of 800 USD is paid after probation for a desk, chair or monitor. A monthly internet reimbursement of up to 40 USD is paid against a bill. Equipment bought with the allowance belongs to the employee after 2 years of service.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days per year with manager approval. Longer stays need approval from HR because of tax and visa rules. Employees must not work from a country where the company has no legal entity without written sign-off.
